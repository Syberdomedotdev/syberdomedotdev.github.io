Paws & Purr Shop

A machine learning–based diagnostic system designed to identify and classify skin diseases in cats using image analysis. The system allows users (such as veterinarians or pet owners) to upload images of a cat’s skin condition, where the model processes the image to detect visible symptoms such as rashes, lesions, redness, hair loss, scabs, or fungal infections. The system utilizes trained deep learning models (such as convolutional neural networks) to analyze patterns in the images and compare them against a dataset of known feline skin diseases.
